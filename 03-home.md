## The Problem

**Section heading:**
## Websites changed. Most platforms didn't.

WordPress changed how the world built websites, and it deserves that legacy. But the internet it was created for in 2003 is not the internet we build for today.

Websites are now applications. They talk to payment systems, CRMs and internal tools. They serve logged-in customers, not just readers. Performance is measured in milliseconds, security is a board-level concern, and development teams work with frameworks, APIs and version control — not FTP.

Most established platforms have spent twenty years adapting to that shift. Magna CMS was designed inside it.

**Transition line:**
> This is what a CMS looks like when you design it now.

## Why Magna CMS

**Section heading:**
## Built on different assumptions.

**Six benefit cards:**

### Designed today
No decades of backwards compatibility steering the architecture. Magna starts from current engineering practice — which means cleaner foundations and fewer inherited compromises.

### Modular by design
A focused core, with everything else as modules and plugins. Your site carries the functionality it uses, not the weight of features you'll never touch.

### Developer-grade
Extending Magna feels like working with a framework, not around a product. Clean structure, sensible APIs, and plugin development that fits contemporary workflows.

### Flexible for business
A company site today, a customer portal next quarter, a full platform next year. Magna doesn't assume what your project becomes.

### Visual page building
Content teams design and publish pages visually — layouts, reusable sections, global headers and footers — without waiting on a developer.

### Security-conscious architecture
Security is a design consideration, not an afterthought: contemporary authentication patterns, controlled extension points, and a core you can reason about.

## Modern Architecture

**For the technically minded:**
Magna is built on a contemporary, framework-based foundation with a modular core, clear extension points and API access. Plugins add functionality without modifying core code, so upgrades stay predictable and customisations stay yours.

## Plugin Ecosystem

**Section heading:**
## Keep the core focused. Add what you need.

Magna's core does content management exceptionally well — and deliberately stops there. Everything else arrives as a plugin or module: installed when you need it, absent when you don't.

The architecture is built for an ecosystem spanning commerce, SEO, forms, membership, CRM integrations, directories and industry-specific modules — some available today, others in development, and an open path for anything you build yourself.

**Three-point strip:**
