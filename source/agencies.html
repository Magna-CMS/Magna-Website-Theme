For Agencies

One platform. Every client project.

Magna CMS gives agencies a reusable foundation: build client sites faster, package what you build once into what you sell many times, and keep control of the stack you're accountable for.

The Agency Problem

Every project shouldn't start from zero

Agencies live with a familiar tension. Custom builds are profitable but slow. Template platforms are fast but generic, and eventually a client asks for something the platform can't do.

Magna is built for the middle path: a dependable core you standardise on, plus real extensibility for the custom work that wins the pitch.

Why Agencies Choose Magna

Delivery that gets faster with every project

Reusable by design

Plugins, content types and themes you build for one client become assets for the next. Your delivery speeds up deliberately, not accidentally.

Custom work on a stable base

Client wants a directory, a portal, a booking flow? Build it as a plugin on the same core you always use instead of a bespoke codebase you'll maintain forever.

Industry solutions you own

Package your vertical expertise in property, hospitality or professional services into modules you deploy repeatedly. Your niche becomes your product.

Clients can actually use it

The admin panel and block editor mean clients manage day-to-day content themselves. Fewer change-request tickets; better margins on retainers.

A stack you control

Open source, Laravel-native, runs on ordinary PHP hosting. No per-seat platform tax, no vendor roadmap dictating yours.

Maintainable across the portfolio

One well-structured platform across many clients beats a museum of one-off builds. Updates, security and developer onboarding all get simpler.

One Core, Two Delivery Modes

Headless when the client needs it. A website when they don't.

Some clients need a Next or Nuxt frontend and a mobile app. Some just need a fast, manageable website. With Magna you standardise on one platform for both: the API serves custom frontends, and the ready-made frontend covers the rest.
