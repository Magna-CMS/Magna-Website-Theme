## The Agency Problem

**Section heading:**
## Every project shouldn't start from zero.

Agencies live with a familiar tension. Custom builds are profitable but slow. Template platforms are fast but generic — and eventually a client asks for something the platform can't do.

Magna is built for the middle path: a dependable core you standardise on, plus real extensibility for the custom work that wins the pitch.

---

## Why Agencies Choose Magna

### Reusable by design
Components, sections and plugins you build for one client become assets for the next. Your delivery gets faster with every project — deliberately, not accidentally.

### Custom work on a stable base
Client wants a directory, a portal, a booking flow? Build it as a plugin on the same core you always use — instead of a bespoke codebase you'll maintain forever.

### Industry solutions you own
Package your vertical expertise — property, hospitality, professional services — into modules you deploy repeatedly. Your niche becomes your product.

### Clients can actually use it
The visual page builder means clients manage day-to-day content themselves. Fewer change-request tickets; better margins on retainers.

### A stack you control
Open source, framework-native, no per-seat platform tax and no vendor roadmap dictating yours. The technology decisions stay where the accountability sits — with you.

### Maintainable across the portfolio
One well-structured platform across many clients beats a museum of one-off builds. Updates, security and onboarding new developers all get simpler.
