## The Story

**Section heading:**
## The web grew up. We kept noticing.

We've spent years building for the web — as developers, and alongside the agencies and businesses that live on it. Over that time, a pattern kept repeating.

The web changed profoundly. Sites became applications. Content became a system. Security became existential. Development matured into a proper engineering discipline, with frameworks, APIs and workflows that would be unrecognisable to 2003.

And yet, so often, projects still began on platforms designed before any of that — brilliant for their time, endlessly patched since, carrying every era of their own history. We watched talented teams spend their best hours working around their tools instead of with them.

The platforms weren't wrong. They were simply from another time. That distinction matters — and it pointed at the question we couldn't put down: *what would this look like if it were designed now?*

---

## What We're Building

**Section heading:**
## A foundation, not just another CMS.

Magna is our answer, built on convictions the last two decades earned:

**Complexity should be chosen, not inherited.** A focused core, with everything else opt-in.

**Developers and content teams both deserve first-class tools.** Neither audience should subsidise the other's experience.

**Extensibility is architecture, not a marketplace.** The ability to build on a platform must be designed in from the first commit.

**Honesty compounds.** We label what's shipped, what's in development and what's planned — because trust is the only durable marketing.

**Open source is the right foundation.** A platform asking for the centre of your web presence should be one you can read, audit and shape.

---

## Where This Goes

**Section heading:**
## Early, ambitious, and building in the open.

Magna is young — we say that plainly. The core is real, the architecture is the point, and the ecosystem is growing into it. What we're building towards is a platform where the question is never *"can the CMS do this?"* but only *"what should we build next?"*

If that's the kind of foundation you want under your work, we'd like you building on it — or with us.
