## How the Ecosystem Works

**Section heading:**
## Four layers, one architecture.

### Core platform
Content management, page building, media, users. Maintained with the platform, deliberately small, and the only layer every site runs.

### Official extensions
Modules built and maintained alongside the core, covering common needs to a consistent standard.

### Custom plugins
What your team builds for your project — first-class citizens of the architecture, upgrade-safe and version-controlled.

### Third-party ecosystem
The open layer: extensions from the community and from agencies packaging their expertise. Open source makes this a design goal, not an afterthought.

## Extension Categories

> Honesty rule for implementation: each listing carries a status — **Available**, **In development**, or **Planned category**. Never present a planned category as a shipped catalogue.

- **Business** — bookings, customer management, operational tools *(planned category)*
- **Marketing** — SEO, forms, campaign and analytics integrations *(in development)*
- **E-commerce** — products, carts and payments on the Magna core *(planned category)*
- **Content** — advanced publishing, workflows, syndication *(in development)*
- **Productivity** — team workflows and admin improvements *(planned category)*
- **Integrations** — connections to CRMs, payment providers and external services *(via custom development today; packaged extensions planned)*

The architecture for all of the above exists today. The catalogue is growing into it — and we'd rather label that honestly than sell you a marketplace that isn't there yet.

## Why This Model

**Section heading:**
## What a focused core buys you.

- **Lighter sites** — projects carry only the functionality they use
- **Clearer security posture** — a small core is easier to audit; extensions are contained by design
- **Predictable upgrades** — extensions built on stable extension points, not core patches
- **No ceiling** — when the ecosystem doesn't cover your need, the build path is open and supported
