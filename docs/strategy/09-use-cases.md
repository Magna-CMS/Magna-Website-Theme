# Start with a website. Don't stop there.

**Supporting paragraph:**
Magna's focused core and extension architecture mean one platform can carry a project from company site to full application. Here's what teams build on it — and why the foundation fits.

---

## Business Websites

The baseline case, done properly: fast pages, a visual builder for the marketing team, and structured content that stays manageable as the site grows.

**Why Magna fits:** the core is purpose-built for exactly this — and when the site's brief grows, the platform doesn't need replacing.

---

## Content Platforms

Publications, blogs at scale, knowledge bases, resource hubs — anywhere content volume and organisation are the product.

**Why Magna fits:** structured content types, reusable layouts and publishing workflows keep large libraries coherent, while the platform stays fast to work in.

---

## Customer Portals

Authenticated areas where customers access documents, account information or services — the point where a website becomes software.

**Why Magna fits:** contemporary authentication patterns and an extensible core make gated, personalised experiences a supported build path rather than a bolt-on.

---

## Membership Platforms

Communities, subscription content, member-only resources — models where access control is the business.

**Why Magna fits:** membership logic lives in extensions on top of a stable content core, so the offering can evolve without re-platforming.

---

## Directories

Listings of businesses, professionals, properties or products — searchable, structured, and often the seed of a marketplace.

**Why Magna fits:** custom content types and modular functionality suit structured, filterable data — and the plugin architecture leaves room for what directories become next.

---

## Marketplaces

Multi-party platforms connecting providers and customers, grown from directory foundations into transactional products.

**Why Magna fits:** the extension architecture supports building transactional and multi-user functionality on the same governed core — custom development, on a foundation designed to carry it.

---

## Internal Business Systems

Intranets, team knowledge bases, operational tools — software your organisation runs on but rarely wants a second tech stack for.

**Why Magna fits:** one platform, already understood by your team, extended inward. Internal tools inherit the same architecture, security posture and workflows as your public site.

---

## Custom Applications

The projects that fit no template at all — industry-specific products and one-of-a-kind platforms.

**Why Magna fits:** a focused core, first-class extension points and framework-native code make Magna a starting point for application development, not an obstacle to it. You're building on the platform, never around it.
