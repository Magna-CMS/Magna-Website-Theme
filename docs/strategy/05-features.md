## Content Management

**Section intro:** The daily work of publishing, made straightforward.

- **Pages & posts** — structured content types for site pages and ongoing publishing. *(Available)*
- **Content organisation** — categories, tags and structure that keep growing libraries navigable. *(Available)*
- **Media management** — upload, organise and reuse images and files across the site. *(Available)*
- **Publishing workflows** — draft, review and publish states so teams ship deliberately. *(In development)*
- **Revisions & scheduling** — content history and timed publishing. *(Planned)*

## Business Features

**Section intro:** One platform across the life of the business.

- **Company websites** — the foundation case: fast to build, easy to run.
- **Portals** — authenticated experiences for customers or partners, built on the same core.
- **Content systems** — publications, knowledge bases and resource hubs at scale.
- **Internal tools** — intranets and team platforms without a second technology stack.
- **Custom platforms** — when the business outgrows "website", the platform doesn't change — it extends.

*These are solution patterns built on Magna's core and extension architecture — not one-click products. See [Use Cases](/use-cases) for how each is approached.*
