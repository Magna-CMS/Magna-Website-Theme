## Design Without Limits

**Section heading:**
## Freedom inside a system.

Page builders usually force a choice: creative freedom that turns into chaos, or rigid templates that turn into sameness. Magna's builder is designed around a third option — flexible layout control operating inside a component system.

Arrange sections, adjust layouts, control spacing and structure — while everything you build stays consistent with your site's design language. The freedom is real. So is the coherence.

---

## Reusable Components

**Section heading:**
## Build once. Use everywhere.

The sections you design — a call-to-action, a pricing band, a testimonial row — become reusable components. Drop them into any page. Update them in one place and the change follows.

That's the difference between building pages and building a design system your whole team can publish with.

- **Sections** you define once and reuse across the site
- **Consistency** that survives dozens of editors and hundreds of pages
- **Speed** — new pages assembled from proven parts, not started from zero

---

## Headers and Footers

**Section heading:**
## Global structure, managed globally.

Headers and footers aren't page content — they're site infrastructure. Magna treats them that way: designed visually, managed centrally, applied everywhere.

Change the navigation once. Every page follows. No hunting through templates, no page-by-page drift.

---

## Responsive Design

**Section heading:**
## One build. Every screen.

Layouts built in Magna adapt across desktop, tablet and mobile. Structure reflows sensibly by default, and you keep control over how designs behave at different sizes — so "responsive" means designed, not merely squashed.

---

## For Designers and Developers

**Section heading:**
## Visual building that doesn't cost you the codebase.

Most visual builders buy convenience with lock-in and markup nobody chose. Magna's builder is part of the platform's architecture, not a layer fighting it.

**For designers:** a component system that protects the design language while giving editors real layout freedom.

**For developers:** builder output that belongs to the same well-structured platform as everything else — extendable with custom components when the standard set isn't enough.

Visual building and engineering discipline aren't opposites here. They're the same system.
