## Two Eras, Two Sets of Assumptions

Platforms inherit the assumptions of their era. A CMS designed in the early 2000s optimised for publishing pages to readers. A CMS designed now assumes websites are applications, teams work like engineering organisations, and requirements change mid-flight.

Neither assumption set is wrong. They're answers to different questions.

---

## The Comparison

| Area | Traditional CMS approach | Magna CMS approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Architecture** | Foundations from an earlier era, evolved carefully over decades | Contemporary, framework-based architecture designed today |
| **Core scope** | Feature-rich cores grown by accretion | Deliberately focused core; everything else modular |
| **Extensibility** | Vast plugin ecosystems of varying quality, compatibility and security | Extension architecture designed in from the start, with contained, upgrade-safe plugins |
| **Development** | Workflows shaped by platform history | Version control, environments and framework conventions as the default path |
| **Applications** | Primarily website-focused, applications possible with effort | Designed for websites *and* the applications they become |
| **Flexibility** | Often shaped by established conventions and themes | Designed for customisation and expansion, with no assumed project type |

## An Honest Assessment

**Section heading:**
## The right platform depends on the project.

If you need the largest possible theme catalogue, a plugin for almost anything, and two decades of community answers to every question — a mature platform like WordPress remains a strong, sensible choice. We won't pretend otherwise.

Magna CMS is designed for a different profile of team:

- Teams who want **contemporary architecture** under long-lived projects
- Teams whose project may **outgrow "website"** — portals, memberships, custom platforms
- Developers who want extension work to feel like **normal software engineering**
- Agencies standardising on a stack they can **reuse, extend and control**
- Businesses that value a **focused, auditable core** over maximum out-of-the-box surface area

If several of those describe you, Magna deserves a serious look.
